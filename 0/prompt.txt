### B1 — Story diagrams (the `sd-*` system)

The narrative beats gained inline line-art diagrams instead of prose alone.

- **Beat 0, stacked** (`.story-diagram--stacked`): `[Femur mesh] + [Surgical plan]` → arrow →

  loop glyph.
- Shared vocabulary: `.sd-ico` (2.4px white stroke, the primary glyph), `.sd-op` (2px at .78
  opacity, secondary detail), `.sd-join` / `.sd-down` (connectors), `.sd-box` (2.4px white
  border, radius 16), `.sd-node` (76px column, uppercase 10px caption).
- Beat 1 also carries a `.story-actions` checklist: *Devin reads failure reports · Rewrites the
  design script · Rebuilds and re-validates the solid · Commits the reasoning change.*

### B2 — Devin brand mark

The `Devin` node's generic rotated-square glyph was replaced with the real Cognition logo
from [artifacts/cognition_logo.png](artifacts/cognition_logo.png), embedded as a
**base64 `data:` URI** so the template stays self-contained (0 external `src=` refs; the page
is served/opened standalone and must not depend on a repo-relative path).

- Source 750×750 RGBA → cropped to artwork (608×698) → resized to 77×88 → 6,158 B PNG →
  8,212 B base64.
- Sits on a `.sd-logo` white rounded tile (58px, 78px at the wide breakpoint). The tile is
  **required, not decoration**: the logo's darkest hex is `#2a6dce` against the story's
  `#5f8bb0` ground ≈ 1.5:1 contrast, so bare on the background its upper-left hexagons
  disappear. The tile also keeps the node the same footprint as its siblings so connectors
  and captions stay aligned.
- The sibling `Test` node reuses the tile with `.sd-brand` — stroke `url(#devin-ramp)`, a linear
  gradient in the Cognition ramp (`#2a6dce` → `#1796e2` → teal) — so the brand colour carries
  across the pair.

### B3 — Bone glyph: two lobes per end

`d="M17.5 17C13.4 18.1 …"` in the beat-0 diagram. The first attempt read as **three** bumps
per end: the segment between the two lobes had control points at y≈8.4 while the lobe crests
were at y≈9.6, so the "notch" arched *higher* than the lobes it separated, making a third,
tallest bump in the middle. Rebuilt so the inter-lobe segment dips to y≈14.1 against crests
at y≈10.8 — two lobes, one notch, mirrored top and bottom about y=28.

### B4 — Tailored plate glyph

- Plate `rect` from `6×22 rx 3.5` to a thinner, longer `5×38 rx 2.5`, so it reads as a plate
  rather than a block and is visibly thinner than the bone shaft.
- The three tick marks that sat *outside* the old plate became three screw holes on the plate
  centreline. They needed their own thin class — a 2px stroke on a 1.3-radius circle renders
  as a solid blob at 44px.
- ⚠️ The requested soft-edge square was first built as `.sd-frame` (48×48 `rx 12`, 1.5px white
  stroke at .34 opacity over a `rgba(255,255,255,.05)` fill, in a 56-unit viewBox). It has since
  been **superseded** by wrapping the two nodes in the existing `.sd-box` border instead;
  `.sd-frame` and `.sd-hole` are gone from the file. The rebuilt bone path survived the swap.

### B5 — Implant reads as metal, not glass

The plate in the workbench viewer looked like transparent glass. Alpha was never the cause —
`toMesh` has always set `alpha: 1`. The cause was tone: the shared bone/implant curve peaked
at a 1.5 multiplier, which pushed the light steel-blue base to a near-white `rgb(215,244,255)`
across a wide band of front-facing triangles.

Two passes on this, and ⚠️ **the second overwrote the first**:

| | Colour | Shading |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | `#8fa3b0` | shared curve, `.42 + .72·l + .36·l⁸` |
| Pass 1 (this session) | `#8b9095` neutral gray | `.54 + .46·l + .26·l¹⁴` — tighter specular, peak 1.26 |
| **Current tree** | `#c8ccd2` light silver | `metalTone(nx,ny,nz,l)` — reflection-vector environment ramp + horizon band + Fresnel edge term |

`metalTone` is the more physical answer and is what the file now calls from the tone
expression in `createMeshRenderer.draw`; the pass-1 constants are gone.
[autoimplants/viewer.py:48](autoimplants/viewer.py#L48) carries the matching colour.

### B6 — Footer, footnote, copy

- New `.page-footer` / `.page-disclaimer` with a `.footnote-ref` superscript from the intake
  assurance line: *"13 geometry checks · 8 stress checks¹"* → research-use disclaimer.
- `.story-kicker` ("Autonomous implant engineering") removed.
- Beat headings rewritten (as committed at HEAD → current tree): *"Start with the anatomy."* → *"Start with
  **your** patient's anatomy."*; *"The agent changes the shape."* → *"Deploy Cognition Devin
  to iteratively improve the plate's shape & fit."*; *"Only validated solids reach review."* →
  *"Agentic loop."* + the sub *"Human oversight reduced to final clinical sign-off."*

### B7 — Dialog cancel could not cancel

`tests/test_viewer.py` gained `test_cancel_buttons_skip_form_validation`: a `<button>` inside a
`<form>` defaults to `type=submit`, so Cancel ran HTML5 validation on the required reviewer
name and acknowledgement checkbox, the submit was blocked, the `onsubmit` router never saw the
submitter, and there was no way out of the approve/revision dialogs without filling in the
decision you were trying to abort. The test pins `value="cancel" formnovalidate` on both
dialogs while keeping the fields required for the real decision paths.
